Status: approved, 2026‑08‑24 · character rendering (§5) is shipped · abilities (§1–4) designed, not yet built

00 — Why A Separate Spec

Movement and combat needed no scoping

Run, jump, wall interactions, roll, combat — all real, shipped, tested. What's genuinely new here is a real ability system, how it gets enhanced, and the character's visual identity. Decomposed deliberately from the room/checkpoint/cinders spec, though the enhancement economy below reuses that spec's own currency pool rather than inventing a third one.

01 — Scope

Character-level, not weapon-specific

Abilities belong to the character, available no matter which of the four weapons ends up equipped. A deliberate choice: weapon switching isn't shipped yet (player.weapon defaults to 'blade', nothing changes it at runtime) — making abilities weapon-specific would have made that a required prerequisite, not an optional one.

Named, not dropped

Weapon-flavored ability variants are real, later, additive design space — deferred until weapon equip/switch exists, the same pattern D8's flask charges/backpack slot already used.

02 — The Two Abilities

Ember Dash & Parry

Ember Dash

A fast horizontal burst, usable in the air — a genuine gap in the current kit. Input: reuses the existing Roll button, context-sensitive (grounded = today's roll, airborne = dash) — zero new bindings, the same pattern Hollow Knight/Dead Cells both use. Carries i-frames, matching Roll's own precedent.

Parry / Deflect

A timed input that negates damage and staggers the attacker. Hooks into the real telegraph Bus event enemies already emit before swinging. Input: genuinely new — nothing in the current 7-action vocabulary has room without an awkward overload.

Real new enemy capability, not a flag flip
The technically riskiest piece, not hidden

"Stagger the attacker" needs 45-enemy.js to support an attack being interrupted mid-telegraph or mid-swing — a real new capability the enemy state machine doesn't have today, not a trivial flag flip.

02b — Touch

A named exception to D10, by player choice

A new settings toggle (same shape reducedMotion/muted already use) lets the player choose: Manual — a new zone added to the locked Gesture Surface layout, or Assist — no new zone, a well-timed dodge gesture near a real telegraph auto-triggers the parry. The one place touch and keyboard/gamepad genuinely play the mechanic differently — by choice, not a forced compromise.

03

Unlock: available from the start

Both abilities are core kit from the very first spawn — no unlock gate, the opposite of D4's blueprint model. Early runs stay complete rather than naked. Meta-currency instead buys the enhancements below.

Enhancements — four flat purchases

05

Character rendering — already shipped

Real code, real gate green — not a proposal

The judged direction (Option A, of three presented) is now real: drawFigure() renders the torso as a gradient between the SAME cloth/clothDark values the flat fill always used, a soft radial glow behind the chest ember, and a low-alpha rim-light on the hood. Zero new colors, zero assets, zero change to the pose rig or its baked hitboxes.

Deliberately not chosen: Option B (painterly overlay, real later option) and Option C (illustrated sprite art — highest ceiling, but breaks the hitbox-baked-from-animation guarantee unless new infrastructure keeps hand-drawn art in sync).

06

Ability VFX — designed, not yet built

Validated as a hand-animated preview, built ON TOP of the §5 baseline above — the ember is already glowing at rest now; these describe how it reacts.

Dash: flare + trail

The chest ember flares brighter mid-dash; a fading ember-colored trail marks the burst — the existing particle system, no new subsystem.

Parry: hood-hollow lights up

The whole hood-hollow lights up on a good read, not just the usual small dim accent — same "flash at the moment of impact" precedent an ordinary hit already uses.
